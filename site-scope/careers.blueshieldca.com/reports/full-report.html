1 · Summary

careers.blueshieldca.com is a 95-URL careers marketing site on the iPR Software newsroom CMS. All job search, job detail and apply traffic goes to Oracle Recruiting Cloud (confirmed as the ATS; the SOW text names Workday). The site has a homepage, 7 section landing pages, a text page, the Our People story hub (77 local stories plus 39 stories cross-posted from news.blueshieldca.com) and 3 legacy account pages. 25 components (38 variations) were observed; per the SOW they consolidate into 4 templates and 17 blocks (12 core) for Blue Shield Careers (the SOW’s Stellarus and Ascendiun cover pages are excluded).

SOW-aligned dev effort: 550 h (≈69 person-days, 13.8 person-weeks), no contingency — see the estimate and SOW alignment.

2 · URL discovery

sitemap.xml returned 97 entries; 2 malformed entries (careers.blueshieldca.comhttps://demo.com, …https://www.blueshieldca.com/) were excluded → 95 URLs. 92 analyzed successfully; /alerts, /confirm and /file return HTTP 404. Job pages are not in the sitemap because they live in Oracle Recruiting Cloud.

7 · Observed behaviors

bsc-header

Desktop (1440px): utility bar, logo, horizontal primary nav and a “Find your next role” button; “Life at Blue Shield” carries a dropdown (ipr_dropdown_2.js) that does not open on hover. Mobile (390px): the nav collapses to a hamburger (“Open Navigation Menu”) that opens an off-canvas “Menu” panel with the same links (sub-items indented), a close (×) button and a full-width “Find your next role” button. That button leaves the site for Oracle Recruiting Cloud.

bsc-poll

“[+] Feedback” (footer-feedback-link) reveals a hidden #poll block with 6 radio questions; submit validates (poll_alert), then shows a success state and results charts from /poll_charts/.

bsc-gallery

Gallery images link to /_gallery/get_file/?file_id=…; clicking opens them in a lightbox (lightGallery / Magnific Popup).

bsc-video-tiles

Tiles link to youtube.com/watch URLs and open a modal YouTube player (Magnific Popup iframe type).

bsc-faq

11 .faq-item entries with .faq-caller headers toggle .faq-body answers (collapsed by default).

bsc-story-listing

GET form (q, c[Role], c[Topic], s, search_type=and) reloads /blog/search with results; ?page=N pagination, 9 tiles per page, 13 pages; 39 tiles link to news.blueshieldca.com.

onetrust

OneTrust banner (“We use trusted partners to improve your experience” + Continue) shows on first visit; OtAutoBlock blocks tags until consent; footer “Cookie preferences” reopens it.

bsc-share

Share buttons open LinkedIn/Facebook/X share dialogs in a new window.

canonical-defect

SEO defect on the live site: 10 key pages (home, belonging, blog, events, growth, hiring, life, total-rewards, why-relocate, authors/search) declare canonical URLs on http://uat.bsccareers.iprsoftware.com — several at legacy slugs (/diversity-equity-inclusion, /university-programs, /our-benefits-and-programs, /workplaces-of-the-future). Fix in EDS and add the legacy slugs to the redirect map.

9 · Recommendations

  • Keep Oracle Recruiting Cloud as the ATS; standardise deep links + UTMs and track apply clicks in CJA. Update the SOW wording from Workday to Oracle Recruiting Cloud.
  • Migrate the SOW baseline of 22 blog articles — BSC to name the 22 stories (or the 55 to exclude) and confirm what the SOW’s 61 content pages refer to.
  • Replace the iPR blog with an EDS query-index story hub (OOTB search + Role/Topic filters, sort, pagination).
  • Drop the iPR account pages, feedback poll and dead job-alerts form; redirect to Oracle Recruiting Cloud (native job alerts / talent community).
  • Fix the UAT canonical defect and include the legacy slugs in the redirect map for BSC.
  • Drop jQuery/Bootstrap/Owl/lightGallery/Magnific — rebuild modals/lightbox/accordion natively for Core Web Vitals and accessibility.
Evidence-based analysis of the live site, the sitemap, the automated page catalog and the SOW.
