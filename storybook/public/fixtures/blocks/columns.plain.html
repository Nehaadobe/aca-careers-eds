Connecting with purpose

Our workplace model is designed around intentional in-person interaction, collaboration, connection, creativity and flexibility. For most teams, this means coming into the office two days each week. For employees with medical conditions that may impact their ability to work in-office, we are committed to engaging in an interactive process and providing reasonable accommodations to ensure their work environment is conducive to their success and well-being.

Our hybrid strategy aligns with our core values

Human: We know that flexibility is a key component to what makes us a great place to do meaningful work.

Honest: We know that intentional cross-collaboration ensures we can provide the best service for our members.

Courageous: Our mission remains at the center of all we do.
