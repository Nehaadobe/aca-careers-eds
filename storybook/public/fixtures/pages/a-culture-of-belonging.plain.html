A Culture of Belonging

We are committed to cultivating a culture of belonging where all employees can fully contribute to meeting the needs of our members. We are human, honest and courageous in eliminating inequities in the communities we serve and in our workplace. This is how we lead as a healthcare system that is worthy of our family and friends and sustainably affordable.

Our employee resource groups

All employees are encouraged to participate in an Employee Resource Group. These groups are employee-led, highly engaging and provide an avenue for service in the communities where we live and work. Each Employee Resource Group partners with executive leaders on strategic initiatives using innovative approaches and perspectives to inform and achieve our mission.

Forty percent of Blue Shield employees belong to one or more employee resource groups. Over the years, these employees have hosted summits, speaker series and served as ambassadors for events such as the Kingdom Day Parade, Pride, Veteran Stand Down, and many more.
