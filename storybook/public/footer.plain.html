Fair hiring practices

Blue Shield of California is an Equal Opportunity Employer (PDF, 489 KB). All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, gender identity, sexual orientation, or protected veteran status, and abides by both Employee Rights (PDF, 2245 KB) and Pay Transparency (PDF, 98 KB) practices. Blue Shield of California participates in the Electronic Employment Verification (PDF, 172 KB).

The Company is committed to providing reasonable accommodations to persons with disabilities throughout the hiring process. Applicants or candidates who need a reasonable accommodation to complete an application, participate in an interview, complete a pre-employment requirement, or otherwise engage in the hiring process should complete this form. If an accommodation is needed for a scheduled interview, the interview may need to be rescheduled to allow time for review. Supporting documentation may be required.
